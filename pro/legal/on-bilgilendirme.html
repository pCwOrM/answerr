Hizmet, ödeme onaylandıktan hemen sonra planın hesabınızda etkinleştirilmesiyle elektronik ortamda anında ifa edilir. Fiziksel teslimat ve kargo yoktur.

  • Sona erme: Yenilemediğiniz plan dönem sonunda ücretsiz Keşif planına geçer. Daha önce Keşif'e geçmek isterseniz bunu konsoldan seçebilirsiniz; ödenmiş dönem sonuna kadar planınız sürer.

2. Main characteristics of the service

answerr is software (SaaS) that compiles written decision policies into explicit rules and returns an ALLOW / REVIEW / DENY verdict for each request. It is used through the API and the console. Plan contents, quotas and features are shown on the Pricing page and in the console when you order.

3. Price and payment

  • Price (0% VAT — Technopark exemption): Developer TRY 1,449.00 a month or TRY 14,388.00 yearly upfront; Pro TRY 4,899.00 a month or TRY 48,588.00 yearly upfront. Abroad and for crypto payments: Developer USD 29 a month (USD 288 a year), Pro USD 99 a month (USD 984 a year). The Seller is an R&D software company at Cukurova University Science Park (Çukurova Üniversitesi Teknokent), so under Law No. 4691 and Temporary Article 20/1 of VAT Law No. 3065 (and the export-of-services exemption for sales abroad) subscription invoices carry 0% VAT. The total you pay is shown in the console and on the payment page before you confirm. There is no delivery cost.
  • When: in advance. A monthly payment gives 31 days of use, a yearly payment 366 days.
  • How: single card charge through the payment institution iyzico (3D Secure), crypto assets on-chain (USDC on Base; USDT on TRON TRC20; USDT / USDC on EVM networks), or bank transfer.
  • Card details: never passed to or stored by the Seller.
  • Overage: only if you enable it: TRY 7.50 per 1,000 decisions on Developer, TRY 5.00 on Pro (0% VAT), billed at the end of the calendar month on actual usage.

4. Performance

The service is performed immediately and electronically by activating the plan on your account as soon as the payment is confirmed. There is no physical delivery or shipping.

5. Term and termination

  • Term and renewal: paid periods do not renew automatically and nothing is charged automatically. We remind you by e-mail seven days before the period ends; if you renew, the new period starts when the current one ends.
  • Termination: a plan you do not renew moves to the free Explore plan at the end of the period. You can switch to Explore earlier in the console; your plan continues until the end of the paid period.

6. Right of withdrawal

Under Article 15/1-(ğ) of the Distance Contracts Regulation, there is no right of withdrawal for services performed immediately in electronic form. At the payment step you agree that performance starts immediately upon payment. The Seller's voluntary refund terms are in Cancellation & Refund.

7. Complaints

  • Where: send complaints to ask@answerr.me or in writing to Balcalı Mah. Güney Kampüs/5 Sk. No: 4/1 İç Kapı No: 26 (Çukurova Üniversitesi Teknokent), 01330 Sarıçam / Adana, Türkiye; they are answered within 30 days at the latest.
  • Consumers: within the monetary limits announced each year by the Ministry of Trade you may apply to Consumer Arbitration Committees, and above them to Consumer Courts; applications can also be made through e-Devlet (TÜBİS).

8. Personal data

Personal data is processed as described in the Privacy notice and the Privacy Policy.

9. Technical steps and errors

  • Ordering: choose the plan and period in the console, save your billing details, accept this form and the Distance Sales Agreement, and pay on the payment page (or submit the transfer / transaction hash).
  • Correcting errors: before confirming payment you can correct your billing details in the console and your card details on the payment page.
  • Keeping the texts: the version you accept is recorded and sent to you with the order confirmation e-mail. The current texts are always on this site.
  • Language: the contract is concluded in Turkish; this English version is a translation for convenience.
