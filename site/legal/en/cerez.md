# Cookie Policy

**Version:** {{legal_version}}

answerr.me does not use advertising or analytics cookies and does not track you across sites.

| Name / place | Provider | Purpose | Duration | Type |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `answerr_pro_lang`, `answerr_lang` (localStorage) | answerr.me | Remember the language you chose | Until you delete it | Preference |
| `answerr_session` (localStorage) | answerr.me | Your console session (console only) | At most 7 days; removed when you sign out | Strictly necessary |
| `answerr_draft_gate`, `answerr_edit_gate` (sessionStorage) | answerr.me | Keep the draft policy you are writing in the compiler while the tab is open | Removed when the tab closes | Strictly necessary / functional |
| `_GRECAPTCHA` and related cookies | Google LLC | Bot protection on forms (reCAPTCHA v3) | Set by Google | Strictly necessary / security |
| Font requests | Google Fonts | Loading the web fonts (no cookie, but Google sees your IP address) | — | Functional |

These cookies and storage are needed for the site to work, for security and for choices you make, so they do not rely on consent. No advertising, analytics or profiling cookies are used; if that ever changes, we will ask for your consent first.

You can delete cookies and local storage in your browser settings; you will then be signed out of the console and your language choice will reset.

Google's processing is covered by the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
