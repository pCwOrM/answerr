# Cancellation & Refund

**Version:** {{legal_version}}

## Cancellation

- You can cancel a paid plan at any time by switching to the Explore plan under **Plan & billing** in the console, or by writing to {{company.email}}.
- Cancellation takes effect at the end of the paid period; your plan continues until then. Paid periods do not renew automatically, so nothing is charged for the next period.

## Voluntary refund

Because the service is performed immediately in electronic form there is no statutory right of withdrawal ([Distance Sales Agreement](mesafeli-satis.html) Art. 6). Even so, ITouch offers the following voluntary refunds:

1. If you cancel **within 14 days of your first paid payment** and have used less than 10% of your plan's monthly quota in that time, we refund the full plan price.
2. If you cancel a yearly plan within the first 30 days, we refund the amount left after deducting the monthly plan price for the months used.
3. Overage fees are not refunded, as they are based on actual usage.
4. If the service is unavailable for more than 24 hours in total in a calendar month for reasons on our side, the plan price for the outage is, at your choice, refunded or added to the end of your paid period.

## How refunds are paid

Refunds are made within 14 days of the request being accepted, by the method used to pay (card or bank account). The refunded period is taken back from your account. The time for a card refund to appear depends on your bank. Any correcting invoice required is issued according to the regulations.

## Requests

Send cancellation and refund requests to {{company.email}} from your account e-mail.
